University Academic Regulations 2025

Attendance Requirements

Students are required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each
registered course during a semester. Students falling below this threshold
will be marked ineligible to sit for the end-semester examination in that
course, unless a medical exemption has been approved by the Dean of Academic
Affairs at least two weeks before the examination period begins.

Examination Eligibility

A student is eligible for the final examination only if they have satisfied
both the minimum attendance requirement and have cleared all outstanding fee
payments by the date specified in the academic calendar. Students who fail to
satisfy the minimum attendance requirement will not be permitted to sit for
the final examination and will be required to repeat the course in a
subsequent semester.

Grading Policy

Final grades are computed using a weighted formula: 30% continuous
assessment, 20% mid-semester examination, and 50% end-semester examination.
A minimum aggregate score of 40% is required to pass a course.

Grievance Procedure

Students who wish to contest a grade must submit a written appeal to the
Examination Cell within 10 working days of the result being published.
Appeals submitted after this window will not be considered.

Leave of Absence

Students requiring an extended leave of absence exceeding two weeks must
submit a formal request to the Registrar, accompanied by supporting
documentation. Approved leaves do not count against the minimum attendance
requirement.
